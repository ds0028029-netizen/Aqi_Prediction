🧪 Pollutant Information

Understand common air pollutants and their effects.

🌫️
PM2.5

Fine particulate matter with a diameter of 2.5 micrometers or smaller. It can penetrate deep into the respiratory system.

💨
PM10

Particles smaller than 10 micrometers. Common sources include road dust, construction and industrial activities.

🏭
CO

Carbon monoxide is a colorless and odorless gas produced mainly by incomplete combustion.

🚗
NO₂

Nitrogen dioxide is associated with vehicle emissions, power generation and combustion.

🏭
SO₂

Sulfur dioxide is produced mainly from burning sulfur-containing fuels and industrial processes.

☀️
O₃

Ground-level ozone is formed through chemical reactions involving pollutants in sunlight.

❤️ Health Advisory

Health recommendations based on the current AQI.

Air Quality

Search an area to receive health information.

Recommended Actions


  • Stay hydrated.
  • Avoid unnecessary outdoor activities during high pollution periods.
  • Keep windows closed when outdoor pollution is high.
  • Use appropriate protection when required.
  • Sensitive individuals should take extra precautions.
  • Check AQI before prolonged outdoor activities.

📢 Public Awareness

Simple actions that can help reduce air pollution.

🚗

Reduce Vehicle Pollution

Use public transportation, carpooling, cycling or walking whenever practical.

🌳

Plant and Protect Trees

Increase green spaces and protect existing trees and vegetation.

🔥

Avoid Open Burning

Avoid burning plastic, garbage, leaves and other waste in open areas.

🏭

Industrial Pollution Control

Industries should use pollution-control equipment and follow environmental standards.

🏠

Improve Indoor Air Quality

Reduce indoor smoke and maintain appropriate ventilation.

📱

Check AQI Regularly

Check air quality before planning long outdoor activities.
